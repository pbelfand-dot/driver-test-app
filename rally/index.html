How to read the notes

  • 3 left: a bend's number, then its direction
  • 6 fastest bend … 1 slowest bend
  • Square ≈ 90° junction turn · Hairpin ≈ 180°
  • tightens / opens the bend gets slower / faster on the way through
  • 100, 200… metres of straight to the next note · into / and right after
  • Caution slow bend at the end of a long straight
  • Lights, Stop sign, Speed camera, Rail crossing, Bump from OpenStreetMap

Notes describe the road's shape, not a safe speed. Obey speed limits and traffic laws, and keep your eyes on the road.
